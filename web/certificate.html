What installing it does

The box made its own certificate authority. Installing it tells your device to trust the box's HTTPS pages, so what you send here (the admin password above all) can't be read or changed by anyone else on the WiFi. It can vouch only for this box: its names () and addresses on the networks it was on when the certificate was made (the hotspot's, and the home or office network's). Your device will not trust it for any other site, even if it were stolen, because those limits are part of the certificate itself. Someone who had taken over the box could, though, use it for another device on those networks: a box you install a certificate from is one you trust.

You don't need it to use the box: every page also works over plain HTTP, as before.
