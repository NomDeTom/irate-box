💬 IRC chat

Real-time chat for everyone on this box's network, in any IRC app.

The box runs a small IRC server. This page is not the chat: it tells your IRC app where to connect. The app has to be on your device already, because there is no internet here to fetch one from.

Connect

🖧
Server: this box

Port 6667. No password, and no TLS/SSL: turn those off if your app asks.

#️⃣
Channel: #lobby

Pick any nickname you like and join #lobby. Your app may open it straight from this link: irc://this box:6667/lobby. If nothing happens, type the server and channel into the app.

Apps people use

  • Android: Goguma, Revolution IRC
  • iPhone and iPad: Textual
  • Windows, Mac and Linux: HexChat, Halloy
  • In a terminal: WeeChat, irssi

Good to know

  • There are no accounts. A nickname is yours only while you are connected, and anyone can take it afterwards.
  • Nothing is encrypted, and only people on this network can see it. Say what you would say aloud in the room.
  • The box keeps no history: you see what is said while you are connected.
  • Other people on the chat see a made-up name for your device, not its address. The same device keeps the same name until the box restarts.
  • #lobby is always there. Any channel you make yourself disappears when the last person leaves it.
  • There are no moderators. The owner can switch the chat off from Admin.
