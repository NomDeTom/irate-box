Rockchip boards

Prepare a Luckfox Lyra over the box's USB. Administrators only.

Prepare a Luckfox Lyra or another RK3506 board for mPWRD-OS: the box erases the board's onboard flash, so that the board starts from its SD card. Connect the board's USB port to a USB port of this box. Connect one board at a time.

Board

Cards in a reader

A board's SD card in a USB card reader on this box: checked without writing to it, tested to show whether it holds as much as it says, or written with a board's image. Close any window the desktop opens for it: a mounted card is not touched.

Boards to set up

A new board joins your network with the choices set here: its network, its accounts, its name. A board with mPWRD-OS raises a setup hotspot at its first start, and shows here while it is in range. Power one new board at a time, near this box: until it is set up, anyone in range can set it up instead.
