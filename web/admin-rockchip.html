Rockchip boards

Prepare a Lyra over the box's USB, and set up new Armbian boards. Administrators only.

Prepare a Luckfox Lyra or another RK3506 board for mPWRD-OS: the box erases the board's onboard flash, so that the board starts from its SD card. You can install the original bootloader or the irate-box NAND U-Boot, and add the Alpine rescue image to a card in a reader. Connect the board's USB port to a USB port of this box. Connect one board at a time.

Installing a bootloader erases the board's onboard flash. If logs were being kept on the NAND, they are erased. There is no migration: the board is prepared again from scratch.

Board images

The librarian mirrors the newest mPWRD-OS and Armbian releases into rk-images, one image for each Lyra board, with the publisher's SHA-256. Cards written below take these images.

Recover a dead Lyra in MaskROM

A Lyra whose NAND boot chain is damaged, or whose U-Boot no longer starts, can be recovered from this page without removing the NAND. Connect the board's OTG0 USB-C to this box (data and power), and its debug UART to a USB serial adapter on this box. The Lyra's console is 1,500,000 baud. Open the serial terminal to watch it.

  1. Power the board off. On the W25N02KV (the 8-pad flash near the SoC), hold pad 6 (CLK) to pad 4 (GND) with fine tweezers or a jumper. Pin 1 is the dot. Pad 6 is the third pad down on the right, pad 4 is bottom-left. Do not touch pad 8 (VCC).
  2. Power the board on while holding the short. When it appears below in MaskROM, release the short at once. If it does not appear, power off, re-seat the short and try again.
  3. In Install a bootloader below, select irate-box NAND U-Boot and Alpine rescue (C). Keep Save a full copy of the onboard flash first ticked. The box reads and saves the whole NAND, then erases it and writes the boot chain, the rescue FIT and the UBI rescue. The board is standalone afterwards: it boots its card first, then USB, then C.

Cards in a reader

A board's SD card in a USB card reader on this box: checked without writing to it, tested to show whether it holds as much as it says, or written with a board's image. Close any window the desktop opens for it: a mounted card is not touched.

New boards

A new board joins your network with the choices set here: its network, its accounts, its name. A Lyra, a Banana Pi or another Armbian board can be set up, over its setup hotspot, its serial console, its SD card or its default password on a cable. Power one new board at a time, near this box: until it is set up, anyone in range can set it up instead.
