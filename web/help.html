🛟 Quick help

What this is, and how to get around.

Theme

Don't panic. You're connected to an Irate-Box: a small computer somewhere nearby, serving these pages over its own network. It needs no internet at all. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere else — it stays on the box, for the people around you.

There are no accounts and nothing to install. Pick any name when a page asks for one.

What's on the box

✏️
Excalidraw — whiteboard

Sketch diagrams by hand. To draw together, use Live collaboration (the people icon) and share the room link. Save to hub gallery in the menu keeps a copy on the box; Export downloads one to your device.

📈
Mermaid — diagrams as text

Type a flowchart, sequence diagram or chart and see it drawn. The Hub tab in History saves to the box. Load note pulls a diagram out of a Notes page. The docs buttons open the Mermaid book in the library.

📚
Kiwix — the offline library

Whole reference books on the box: a selection of Wikipedia, the Meshtastic and Mermaid documentation, and whatever else the owner has added. Search covers every book at once.

📝
Notes — a shared notebook

Markdown pages everyone here can read and edit. Good for agendas, lists, and anything worth keeping after the room empties.

🔀
Git — repositories

Browse the box's public repositories and clone them over the network: git clone http://<this box>/git/<name>.git. Pushing needs the owner's login unless they have opened it to everyone.

📡
Meshtastic

A log analyser for Meshtastic radios, their documentation, and tools. Saved logs work in any browser, here. Reading a radio live over USB needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop, and the downloaded copy (Meshtastic → "Serial terminal, as a download"): browsers only allow USB serial on a page opened from your own machine, not on one served over this box's plain connection. Save it once, open it from your downloads, and plug the radio in.

🧮
Calculators

RF and LoRa, electronics, and general converters — all working offline.

💬
Shoutbox and board

On the front page. The shoutbox is quick chat; messages fade after a day. The board holds threads, which fade a week after their last reply.

🗨️
IRC chat

Live chat in any IRC app, if the owner has added it. How to connect: the app has to be on your device already, as there is no internet here to get one.

📥
File drop

Leave a file for everyone else here, up to 25 MB, and pick up theirs. Files fade after a day of the box being on, and anyone on this network can download them.

Getting around

  • The tiles on the front page open each app in a new tab, under a thin hub bar: 🏠 Hub brings you back, 🛟 shows a short reminder of all this, ☀️ 🌙 sets light or dark for everything, and ↗ opens the app on its own. The bar slides away as you scroll down.
  • A greyed-out tile is a service that isn't running right now, or isn't installed on this box.
  • If a button inside an app says it can't connect, it is usually a feature that needs the internet, switched off here on purpose.
  • To bring someone else in, show them the Join QR code on the front page.

Your work

  • Most apps keep what you're doing in your browser. It's still there next time you come back on the same device, and nobody else sees it.
  • Saving to the hub (the gallery, Notes, a shared drawing) puts it on the box, where everyone connected can see it.
  • Space on the box is limited, and the oldest shared work makes room for new. Download anything you want to keep.

Privacy and manners

  • Everything is anonymous: the box doesn't know who you are, only the name you type.
  • Anything shared here can be read by anyone on this network. Don't post what you wouldn't pin to a noticeboard.
  • The box's owner can remove posts and saved work.

Why times look odd

The box has no clock battery, so it doesn't always know the date. Ages like "5 min ago" count the time the box has been switched on, so a message from yesterday can look newer if the box was off overnight.

For the owner

Admin (password) has the services, the library, moderation, saved work and settings.
Services shows what is running.
